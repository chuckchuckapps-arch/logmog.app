Fully On-Device

All data lives on your iPhone in SwiftData. No accounts, no servers, no subscriptions — ever.

Your data stays on your phone.

LogMog collects nothing and sends nothing. Every log, every workout, every measurement is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. On-device AI via Apple FoundationModels means even your coaching tips never leave your device.
