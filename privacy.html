Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 12, 2026

Short version: LogMog stores all your data locally on your device. We collect nothing, transmit nothing, and sell nothing.

1. Who we are

LogMog is an iOS application developed by Chuck Silver. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at chuckchuckapps@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect no personal data. LogMog has no backend server, no account system, and no analytics SDK. Everything you enter — your weight, meals, workouts, sleep, and health metrics — is stored exclusively on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework.

3. Apple HealthKit

With your explicit permission, LogMog reads the following data types from Apple Health:

  • Step count (today's total)
  • Sleep analysis (last night's duration)

This data is displayed on your dashboard and is never transmitted off your device. LogMog does not write any data to Apple Health. We do not share HealthKit data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

4. Local notifications

If you grant permission, LogMog schedules local notifications (daily logging reminders and weekly plan reminders). These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device by iOS. No notification content is sent to any server.

5. Third-party services

LogMog uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in the app.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with anyone — because we never have access to it in the first place.

7. Data retention & deletion

All app data is stored in your device's local SwiftData store. You can delete all data at any time by deleting the LogMog app from your iPhone. Deleting the app permanently removes all associated data from your device.

8. Children's privacy

LogMog is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app's release notes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions or concerns? Email us: chuckchuckapps@gmail.com
