Privacy Policy

Effective: August 2026

Overview

BoxPeach is a breathing and mindfulness app. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Data We Collect

BoxPeach stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Breathing session records (date, duration, rounds completed)
  • App settings and preferences (phase durations, sound, haptics)
  • Streak and session history

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable Apple Health sync (see below).

Apple Health Integration

If you choose to enable Apple Health Sync, BoxPeach will write mindful session data to the Apple Health app on your device. This data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own Health settings. BoxPeach does not read any Health data from your device.

Analytics & Third Parties

BoxPeach does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. We do not share any data with third parties.

Push Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, BoxPeach requests notification permissions to deliver local notifications on your device. These notifications are scheduled locally and no data is sent to any server.

Data Retention

All data is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Deleting the app removes all associated data. You can also clear your session history from within the app at any time.

Children's Privacy

BoxPeach does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
